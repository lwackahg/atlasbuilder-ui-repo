# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-10-24

First Thunderstore release.

- Generate Riverheim maps from a seed with a click; several seeds and map styles at once, with a queue.
- **Installed Riverheim:** maps are drawn by the Riverheim in your profile, so they match your game (AtlasBuilder 1.0.0 bundles an older Riverheim).
- **Custom world settings:** every Riverheim Config 3.0 setting, with search, import from and export to the mod's config file.
- Captions, a settings file next to every map, and Map history to find old maps and reload their settings.
- Finds AtlasBuilder in its own mod-manager folder, and Riverheim, BepInEx and Valheim automatically.
